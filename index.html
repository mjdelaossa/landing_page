A field guide to the small things

A world in
every flower.

One small visitor. A thousand quiet exchanges. Come closer to the life that keeps the landscape in motion.

01 / A small beginning

Not a mascot.
A whole world.

Meet the bee as it is: a remarkable worker, a navigator, and a living thread connecting one bloom to the next. Its story is written across the places we share.

An invitation to look closer

02 / A map made in motion

The shortest
way is curious.

A bee's route is no ruler-straight commute. It loops, doubles back, and remembers the promising places. Every bend is a small act of discovery.

A route, never a straight line

One tiny route / many possible stops

03 / The exchange

A visit that
changes everything.

Nectar fuels the flight. Pollen travels along. Flower by flower, the exchange becomes the beginning of seeds, fruit, and another season.

A little pollen
goes a long way

04 / A different kind of color

The garden
is not the same
garden.

Bees notice patterns and ultraviolet marks that we can't see. A flower can hold a hidden landing strip, guiding its visitor toward the nectar.

A world beyond our palette

Human viewUV / visible to bees

05 / Many, moving as one

A hive is
a living verb.

No single bee carries the whole plan. Signals, shared labor, and thousands of small decisions make a colony hum. Cooperation, made visible.

A collective built one visit at a time

06 / When the pattern shifts

A quieter
kind of spring.

Fewer wild places, fewer kinds of flowers, and a changing climate can leave pollinators with less to work with. The signs are different from place to place. The response can be, too.

07 / Leave a little room

A wilder
edge is a start.

Plant what belongs where you live. Leave a few stems standing. Let a corner flower before you tidy it. Small gestures add up when a whole neighborhood makes them.

More habitat, less fuss
